==================================================
  HOW IT WORKS
==================================================

STEP 1: Enter ERP Login
   - Enter your ERP username and password
   - Click "Continue"

STEP 2: Upload Timetable
   - Fill in student details (Year, Semester, Division, Batch)
   - Upload your weekly timetable (Excel/CSV)
   - Click "Start Analysis"

STEP 3: Wait for Processing (Automatic)
   - App logs into ERP and fetches attendance
   - Gemini AI processes your timetable
   - All happens in background!

STEP 4: View Dashboard
   - See overall and subject-wise attendance
   - Get notifications for low attendance
   - View attendance calendar

STEP 5: Use Bunking Buddy
   - Select days you want to bunk
   - See real-time attendance impact
   - Plan safe bunk days!
